# Kinstamp design system

Kinstamp — USCIS case tracking for families and their attorneys. The brand name joins *kin* (family) and *stamp* (the official USCIS stamp). The interface pairs a warm name with a sober, precise look: calm, trustworthy, document-like, never a government website and never playful.

The Stitch screens were a starting point. This document describes what is built in `src/` and is the reference for new screens. Tokens live in `src/app/globals.css`; primitives in `src/components/ui/` (shadcn/ui on Base UI, restyled) and `src/components/status/`; every component is visible with sample data at `/design` (development only).

## Principles

- **Black leads.** Primary buttons, the signed-in top bar, active states and headings use deep black `#0B0B0F` (hover `#1A1A22`). Everything else is light: canvas `#F6F6F8`, cards `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E6E6EA` border and an almost invisible offset shadow.
- **One accent, used sparingly.** "Stamp vermilion" `#E5532D` for marks: the logo dot, the current step of a timeline, the "Changed" chip, the "your case" marker on charts, the focus ring and the text caret. Vermilion text uses the darker `#C43A1B` (5.3:1 on white). Never large vermilion areas, never two vermilion elements competing on a screen.
- **Calm by default.** Generous spacing, short copy, fixed type scale. Loading is a thin black progress line or a skeleton, never a spinner in the middle of content.
- **Status is text plus color, never color alone.** A solid dot and the USCIS status title; the tone name is also announced to assistive tech.
- **Honest copy.** Product language from `GLOSSARY.md`. No invented data (no "faster than 72%", no jurisdictions), no legal-sounding jargon, and a "not legal advice" note wherever Kinstamp explains a status.

## Typography

One family carries the whole interface, Apple-style: **Inter 4 (variable)** loaded with `next/font` and its **optical-size axis (`opsz` 14–32)** enabled. The browser picks the text cut below ~20px and the display cut above it, so large titles get the tighter, crisper letterforms SF Pro Display gives on Apple platforms while small UI text keeps open apertures. Rendering is identical on macOS and Windows because the font is self-hosted. Features `cv11` and `ss03` are on for a single-storey `a` and rounder quotes.

Why Inter over the system font: `system-ui` renders as SF Pro on a Mac and Segoe UI on Windows, so reviewers would not see the same product. Inter is the closest widely available cousin of SF Pro, has true optical sizing, nine weights, tabular figures and full Latin coverage for Spanish.

Receipt numbers and other identifiers use **JetBrains Mono** (`font-mono`), grouped for reading: `EAC 999 910 3403`. Monospace is for data, never for decoration.

Fixed rem scale (ratio ≈ 1.2) with size-specific tracking:

| Utility | Size / line | Tracking | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `text-display` | 48 / 1.08 (56 on landing) | −0.028em | 600 | Landing hero |
| `text-title` | 28 / 34 | −0.02em | 600 | Page titles |
| `text-heading` | 20 / 26 | −0.012em | 600 | Section and dialog titles |
| `text-subheading` | 16 / 24 | −0.012em | 600 | Card titles |
| `text-md` (body) | 15 / 24 | −0.004em | 400 | Body and controls |
| `text-sm` | 14 / 20 | 0 | 400 | Secondary text |
| `text-caps` | 12 / 16 | +0.06em | 600, uppercase | Small section labels |

Numbers that align (dates, days, counts) use `tabular`.

## Color

Brand: ink `#0B0B0F`, ink-hover `#1A1A22`, ink-muted `#5C5C66` (6.6:1), ink-subtle `#767680` (placeholders, 4.6:1), ink-disabled `#A0A0A8` (disabled only), canvas `#F6F6F8`, canvas-deep `#EDEDF0`, surface `#FFFFFF`, line `#E6E6EA`, line-strong `#CFCFD6` (inputs), stamp `#E5532D`, stamp-ink `#C43A1B`, stamp-tint `#FDEEE9`.

Status tones, each ≥ 4.5:1 on white so the same shade works as a dot and as text:

| Tone | Categories | Color |
|---|---|---|
| In progress | received, biometrics, interview, evidence_received, transferred | blue `#2563EB` |
| Action needed | evidence_requested | amber `#B45309` |
| Approved | approved, card_produced, card_delivered | green `#15803D` |
| Denied | denied, withdrawn | red `#B42318` |
| Not checked yet | other / no category | gray `#6B6B75` |

Each tone has a `-tint` background (`#EAF1FE`, `#FDF3E6`, `#E8F6EE`, `#FBEBE9`, `#F0F0F3`) used only behind small chips with the tone's text color on top. No pastel pills with same-color text, no gradients, no glow, no glassmorphism, no indigo or purple anywhere.

## Shape, space, depth

- Radii: 8px controls (`rounded-lg`), 12px cards and dialogs (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl` for overlays), full for chips and the language switch.
- 4px grid; cards pad 20px on mobile and 24px on desktop. Page content is 1120px wide, centered, with one page gutter token: `--gutter` (16px on phones, 24px from `sm`), applied with the `gutter-x` utility by `Container` and reused by dialogs (`calc(100% - 2 * var(--gutter))`).
- Phones: every tap target is at least 44px. Compact button sizes (`sm`, `xs`, `icon-sm`, `icon-xs`) grow to 44px below `sm` and shrink from there; the language switch and account menu do the same. Small inline controls that must stay visually small (copy receipt, "Read in Spanish") use the `hit-area` utility, which adds an invisible 44px hit box. Safe-area insets are exposed as `--safe-top` / `--safe-bottom` (`pt-safe`, `pb-safe`) with `viewport-fit: cover`; full-height overlays use `dvh`, never `vh`.
- Sticky offset: `--header-offset` (100px on phones where the nav has its own row, 56px from `md`) feeds a global `scroll-margin-top` on every `[id]`, so anchors and `scrollIntoView` land below the sticky bar.
- Elevation is declared once: cards use border + `shadow-card` (1px offset, soft); hover and auth cards use `shadow-raised`; dialogs and menus use `shadow-overlay`. Never a zero-offset colored halo.
- Focus: 2px vermilion ring with 2px offset on every interactive element (white ring on the black top bar).

## Motion

≤ 300ms, `ease-out-expo` for entrances and `ease-standard` for state changes. One entrance per screen (`fade-up` on the page content), never per card. Hover and press feedback is immediate (`active:scale-[0.985]` on buttons). Everything is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The one ambient exception is the auth backdrop (see Components), which is slow (4s and 24s loops), low contrast and static under reduced motion.

The case-list page swap (`.case-page` in `globals.css`) is the one choreographed transition: the leaving page fades out and slides 10px toward the direction of travel over `--duration-fast` (`ease-standard`), while the entering cards fade in from 10px on the other side over `--duration-base` (`ease-out-expo`) with a 30ms stagger per card (`--index`), so a full page of 5 settles in about 320ms although no single card moves for more than 200ms. Direction comes from a `data-direction` attribute (`forward` / `backward`, or `none` for a fade in place when the search changes the result set) and a changing React `key`; the first paint has no direction and does not animate, and a URL change that leaves the same cards on screen does not swap at all. Both lists share one grid cell so the pager does not jump mid-swap. Under reduced motion the global rule shortens the durations and the stagger is zeroed, so the swap is instant.

## Components

- **Buttons:** primary black 44px (`lg` 48px with 12px radius for the main call to action), secondary canvas with a 1px black border, outline, ghost, destructive (white with red border, fills red on hover), link, inverse (white on black). Icon on the left, 16px.
- **Inputs:** 44px, `line-strong` border, vermilion ring on focus, red border when `aria-invalid`. Labels are always visible; the error replaces the hint under the field and is announced with `role="alert"`.
- **StatusIndicator:** 8px dot + status title (12px dot at `lg`).
- **ReceiptNumber:** mono, grouped, optional copy button with "Copied" feedback.
- **Timeline:** date column (tabular) · dot column (vermilion for the current step, black for earlier ones, 1px line) · title with an origin label ("Reported by USCIS" / "Observed by Kinstamp"), optional description, "New" chip for the event just observed. On phones the date column folds above the title, next to the dot, so the text keeps its full width.
- **Banner:** white, 1px status rail on the left, icon in the tone color, title + description + optional action. Used for "USCIS is offline (Mon–Fri, 7am–8pm ET). Showing the last saved status from …".
- **EmptyState:** dashed border on the canvas, icon tile, heading, one sentence, one primary action.
- **PageHeader:** breadcrumbs → title → meta line (receipt, form) with actions on the right. The actions keep the top-right slot on phones too (an icon button or one short primary button), so the title row never pushes the action below the fold.
- **StatusHero (case detail):** the lead card of a case. Caps label + "Read in Spanish" toggle, `StatusIndicator` at `lg`, the plain-language meaning as a `text-lg` lead, the official USCIS text under a "What USCIS says" caps label behind a 1px hairline rail, the "not legal advice" note, a "Checked … · Last change …" line, and a `CardFooter` action bar: outline group (Share with attorney, Ask about this case) on the left, black Refresh with its outcome line on the right. On phones Refresh comes first and every action is full width.
- **Case detail layout:** a header band (title, receipt, form, the header actions on the right and an optional second row, closed by a hairline) → offline banner → StatusHero → two columns from `lg` (main: "What usually comes next", Timeline; aside: My notes (attorneys), Key dates, Shared with, Is my case slow?, Insight). Below `lg` the cards form one column ordered by what the reader needs first: notes and Key dates right after the hero, then for clients next steps before the Timeline and for attorneys the Timeline before the general next steps, then Shared with, slow check, insight. Cards are never nested and all share the 24px grid gap; the sticky top bar never covers content (`--header-offset`).
- **SharedCasesTable (attorney):** a table from `md` (880px minimum, horizontal scroll inside its card); below `md` each row becomes a card (nickname, receipt, client, status, last change, next key date) that links to the case, like `CaseCard`. Next to the summary line, a segmented "All (N) / Needs review (N)" control (same look as the language switch, `role="radiogroup"`, full width on phones; a 6px vermilion dot before "Needs review" while N > 0). A row that needs review (`src/lib/attorney/review.ts`) gets `NewUpdateMark` (8px vermilion dot + "New update" in `stamp-ink` `text-xs` semibold, never the dot alone) under the receipt, and an outline `xs` "Mark as reviewed" (check icon) in a last, unlabeled actions column; on phone cards the mark and the button sit in a hairline footer under the link. No row background tint. The default order ("Most urgent first") puts rows that need review first, then the nearest key date. Marking is optimistic (`useOptimistic`): the mark, the button and the count clear at once and the server confirms on refresh. "Needs review" with nothing left shows an inline `EmptyState` ("You're up to date").
- **SearchBar (case list, `CaseSearch`):** above the summary line, full width on phones and 360px from `sm`; the same field as the attorney filters (44px, 40px from `md`, search icon on the left, `type="search"` with the native cancel button hidden). While empty, a `/` `kbd` hint sits on the right from `sm` and the `/` key focuses the field whenever nothing else takes keystrokes; with text, a ghost icon button (44px on phones) clears it, as does Esc. Placeholder "Search by nickname, receipt or status". Typing is debounced 150ms and written to the URL as `?q=` (dropping `?page=`, so a search starts on page 1); the field keeps its own draft so reload keeps both. Matching is pure (`src/lib/cases/search.ts`): case- and accent-insensitive, receipts match without spaces or dashes, statuses match in either language, every word must match somewhere. The summary keeps counting every case and gains "4 of 9 cases match" (a polite `role="status"`, on its own line on phones); the pager pages the filtered results and hides when they fit on one page. No match shows an inline `EmptyState` in a card ("No cases match “query”", one sentence, outline "Clear search"); the "No cases yet" empty state and the absence of a search bar are for accounts without cases.
- **CasePager (case list):** 5 cases per page, shown only when there is more than one page. "Page 1 of 2" in muted `text-sm` with tabular figures between two icon buttons (lucide chevrons). The full pager sits centered under the list with `outline` `icon-sm` buttons (44px on phones, 36px from `sm`); a compact `ghost` `icon-xs` copy sits at the right of the summary line and is hidden below `sm`. Arrows are disabled at the ends and carry `aria-label`s; only the full pager is an `aria-live` region, so a page change is announced once. The summary line keeps counting every case. The current page lives in the URL (`?page=2`, omitted for page 1), written with the native History API so Next syncs `useSearchParams` without a server round trip; out-of-range or malformed pages clamp to the nearest valid one (`src/lib/cases/paging.ts`), which also covers a page that empties when a case is removed. Changing page from the bottom pager brings the list top back under the sticky header; nothing else scrolls.
- **Dialog:** centered card from `sm`; on phones it is a bottom sheet (full width, rounded top, slides up, scrolls inside `100dvh - 2rem`, safe-area bottom padding). Alert dialogs stay centered at both sizes. The assistant Sheet is full width on phones with safe-area padding on its header and composer.
- **KeyDateChip:** countdown with urgency: past (gray), today (red), within 7 days (amber), later (neutral).
- **KeyDatesCard (case detail):** a preview, not the whole list. Title "Key dates" with a one-line `CardDescription` summary ("2 this month · next in 3 days"; "None this month · next in 40 days"; "Nothing coming up" when nothing is upcoming) and "Add a key date" fixed in the top-right `CardAction` (outline `sm`; icon-only 44px with an `aria-label` below `sm`). Rows (`KeyDateRow`) are a three-column grid that never reflows: a 48px date tile on the left (caps month, `text-heading` day, small weekday, all tabular, formatted in UTC so the server's zone never shifts the day), the body in the middle (title; countdown chip beside "Due date / Appointment · 9:30 ET"; notes clamped to two lines with a More / Less link when they are long), and a fixed actions column on the right (ghost `icon-sm` add-to-calendar menu and delete, 44px on phones, aligned with the title line). The card shows only the three nearest upcoming rows, the soonest with a black date tile; nothing expands inline. The `CardFooter` is one quiet full-width ghost button, "See all key dates (N)", that opens the calendar view, where the rest and every past date live. Deleting asks for confirmation in a centered alert dialog ("Delete this key date?", ghost "Keep it", destructive "Delete"). Grouping and the summary are pure (`src/lib/cases/key-dates.ts`, `key-dates-calendar.ts`); every row renders on the server and is handed to the client calendar, so the delete and calendar controls stay wired in both places.
- **KeyDatesCalendar (`src/components/cases/key-dates-calendar.tsx`):** the "See all" view. The standard `Dialog` (bottom sheet on phones, here full height `100dvh - 2rem`; centered from `sm` at 880px wide and a fixed `min(680px, 100dvh - 4rem)` tall so flipping months never resizes it). Header: `text-heading` "Key dates", the case nickname or receipt as the description, then a row with the month navigation (ghost `icon-sm` ‹ ›, the month and year as a polite live heading, an outline `xs` "Today" disabled on the current month) and a `role="status"` summary of the visible month ("2 key dates in November · 1 due date, 1 appointment"). Body: the grid on the left (420px from `sm`, with a hairline between the columns) and the agenda on the right, scrolling on its own; stacked and scrolling together on phones. The grid is a hand-built `table role="grid"` (no calendar library) of six fixed weeks, Sunday-first in English and Monday-first in Spanish (`weekStartFor`), caps weekday headers, 40px cells (44px on phones), days outside the month in `ink-subtle` and inert, today with a 1px inset ink ring and semibold, the selected day filled black. Days with key dates carry one 6px dot per kind under the number: vermilion for due dates, ink for appointments (white on the selected day), with a legend under the grid; the dots are decorative and every cell has an `aria-label` like "Tuesday, November 3, 1 due date, 1 appointment" (today adds ", today"). Cells are toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`): selecting a day filters the agenda to it, selecting it again (or the `xs` ghost "Show the whole month") clears. One roving tab stop; arrows move by day and week, Home/End to the ends of the month, PageUp/PageDown flip the month and keep the day of month (clamped), Enter/Space select. The agenda lists the month's (or the day's) rows as the same `KeyDateRow`s with their actions, past ones muted; an empty month says "Nothing planned in November" with a link button "Jump to January 2027" to the next month that has a date, and an empty selected day says "Nothing planned on …". It opens on the month of the next upcoming key date (today counts), else the current month. A month flip re-keys the grid and the agenda, which slide in 12px from the direction of travel while fading (`.month-swap` in `globals.css`, `--duration-base`, `ease-out-expo`; nothing moves on first paint and reduced motion makes it instant). The date logic (month matrix per week start, grouping by day and month, kind counts, next month with dates, initial month, preview summary) is pure in `src/lib/cases/key-dates-calendar.ts` with Vitest coverage.
- **Attorney case detail extras:** (1) `ClientIdentity` as the second row of the header band: a 40px initials disc on `canvas-deep` (the one place initials are used; `initialsOf` in `src/lib/format/initials.ts`), the name in `text-md` semibold with an sr-only "Client:" label, the email and "Shared since …" in muted `text-sm` (the date hides on phones), and an outline `sm` "Write to client" `mailto:` link on the right (icon-only, 44px, labelled, on phones). (2) `ReviewControl` in the PageHeader actions at every width, rendered once: "New update" mark + outline `sm` "Mark as reviewed" (icon-only 44px with an `aria-label` below `sm`); a muted check + "Reviewed" once cleared, announced as `role="status"`. (3) `AttorneyNotes` ("My notes", first in the aside and right after the hero on phones): the title carries a muted tabular count, the description is a lock icon + "Only you can see these. Your client can't.", then a one-line composer (44px, `useAutoGrowTextarea`) that grows to four lines while typing and scrolls inside after that; Enter adds a line, Ctrl/⌘+Enter saves, and the "Save" row (shortcut hint on the left, black `sm` "Save" on the right) only exists while there is text (`.note-reveal`). Under it the three most recent notes as full-bleed rows (`src/lib/attorney/notes-view.ts`: `NOTES_PREVIEW`, `splitNote`): first line in `text-md` semibold as the title, then one muted line with the relative time · the rest of the note, both truncated, so every row is 68px and the card never grows past three rows. A saved note folds in at the top (`.note-row[data-inserted]`, `grid-template-rows` 0fr → 1fr) while the fourth row folds away (`[data-leaving]`); the draft clears at once and comes back if saving fails. The `CardFooter` is the quiet full-width ghost "See all notes (N)" when N > 3. Rows and the footer open the notes view. Empty: one muted sentence with the example "Ask for the marriage certificate".
- **NotesView (`src/components/attorney/wired/notes-view.tsx`):** every note of the case, iPhone Notes style. The standard `Dialog` (full-height bottom sheet on phones, centered 880 × `min(680px, 100dvh - 4rem)` from `sm`, like the calendar). Header: `text-heading` "My notes", the case nickname or receipt as the description, an outline `sm` "New note" (icon-only on phones) and the close button. From `sm` two panes: on the left (300px, `canvas/60`, hairline on the right) a 36px search field and the notes grouped "Today / Previous 7 days / Earlier" by last edit (`groupNotesByRecency`; `matchesNoteQuery` is case- and accent-insensitive and needs every word) as a `role="listbox"` of options (title, relative time · preview; the open note filled black); on the right the note in a plain editor: a toolbar (`role="status"` "Created / Edited …", "Saving…", "Saved"; ghost delete) over a borderless textarea whose toolbar hairline turns vermilion while it has focus. Autosave on blur, Ctrl/⌘+Enter and Esc; an empty new note is simply dropped when left; edits, adds and deletes are optimistic (`useOptimistic` reducer) and the new note's UUID is minted in the browser so the optimistic row and the saved note are one. On phones the list and the note are two pages that slide in 16px from the direction of travel (`.notes-pane[data-direction]`), the note page with a ghost "‹ All notes" back button and no selection highlight. Keyboard: one roving tab stop in the list, arrows and Home/End move it (from `sm` the editor follows), Enter opens the note (focus to the editor from `sm`), ArrowDown from the search field enters the list, Esc clears a search before it closes the dialog, focus returns to the opener. Opening from a preview row lands on that note (editor focused from `sm`, back button on phones); from "See all notes" on the newest note from `sm` and on the list (search focused) on phones. Empty: inline `EmptyState` with "New note"; no match: "No notes match “…”" with a ghost "Clear search". Delete asks in the centered alert dialog ("Delete this note?", ghost "Keep it", destructive "Delete"). Reduced motion makes every step instant.
- **Cards:** one level only, never nested. Section labels in `text-caps`.
- **Top bar (signed in):** black, 56px, wordmark left, nav center (underline on the active item), language switch and account menu right. On mobile the nav moves to a second 44px row. Both bars are sticky (`StickyHeader`, `sticky top-0 z-40`) with a translucent backdrop (`bg-ink/85` or `bg-canvas/80` + `backdrop-blur-md`, solid where `backdrop-filter` is unsupported) and a hairline (`border-white/10` / `border-line`) that only appears once the page has scrolled, via the `useScrolled` hook (`useSyncExternalStore` on `scroll`, server-rendered as not scrolled). Overlays sit above at `z-50`.
- **Language switch:** small segmented EN / ES control, present on every screen.
- **Help button:** a "?" ghost icon button in the signed-in top bar, before the language switch. It replays the guided tour of the current page and is absent on pages without one (Insights, Settings).
- **Tour (`src/components/tour/`):** hand-rolled spotlight tour, no library. A shade (`ink` at 55%) with a cutout around the target, drawn as a rounded box whose box-shadow darkens everything else, plus a 2px vermilion ring; the cutout follows the target on scroll and resize with the `slow` ease-out transition. The card is a white `rounded-2xl` `shadow-overlay` popover (360px) below the target, else above, else beside it; on phones it is a bottom sheet with safe-area padding. Caps step counter ("Step 2 of 5"), `text-heading` title, muted body, "Skip tour" link on the left, Back / Next (Done) on the right. Rendered in a portal on `<body>` (the page entrance leaves a transform on the content wrapper, which would otherwise trap the fixed layers). Focus moves to the primary button, Tab stays inside the card, Esc skips, arrows navigate, a polite live region announces each step, and focus returns to the opener. Targets are `data-tour` attributes; steps whose target is missing (empty state, other role) are skipped. One entrance (fade) for the whole tour, nothing per step. Tours are defined in `tours.ts`, copy under `tour.*` in the messages, and completion is stored per account in `profiles.tours_completed`.
- **IdleTimeoutDialog:** centered alert dialog ("Still there?") after 30 minutes without pointer, keyboard or touch activity: one sentence with the seconds left, a 4px black line that shrinks over the minute (it steps once per second under reduced motion), and two actions: outline "Sign out now" and black "Stay signed in" (focused). The countdown is anchored to the moment the warning appears, so a laptop that slept through the limit wakes up to the dialog with a full minute. When it runs out, the person lands on sign-in with a quiet notice (`FormNotice`: canvas background, info icon, muted text, `role="status"`) explaining why.
- **Logo:** "Kinstamp" in Inter 600 with tight tracking and the stamp mark: a postmark ring with a check, open at the top right where the vermilion ink dot sits (`StampMark`). Strokes are 2.25 on a 24px grid so it reads at 16px (favicon, `src/app/icon.svg` + `favicon.ico`) and at 160px; `monochrome` renders the dot in `currentColor` for tinted tiles. Two alternates (`StubMark`, a receipt stub with a vermilion perforation; `TimelineMark`, three stages with the latest in vermilion) and the previous rounded-square mark (`LegacyMark`) are kept only in the gallery for comparison. The email template draws the mark as a CSS circle with the dot, since a check cannot be drawn reliably in email clients.
- **AuthBackdrop:** backdrop for sign-in, sign-up and check-email. A 48px ink grid at 4.5% opacity fading toward the edges, a dotted "case path" with four stage dots at 22%, a pulsing vermilion ring on the last stage and a small vermilion stamp ring that travels the path over 24s with a CSS motion path (`offset-path`). Pure SVG + CSS, no library, `aria-hidden`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the global rule freezes both animations on their last frame; browsers without `offset-path` show the traveller at rest. Light mode only, like the rest of the app. The only exception to "one entrance per screen": it is ambient, not an entrance, and never competes with the form.

## Do not use

Gradients, glows, colored shadows, glassmorphism, illustrations and emoji, pastel pills, initials as avatars anywhere but the client identity disc, light gray text on white, serif display type, colored left rails thicker than 1px, modals for tasks that do not need focus, animations longer than 300ms, and any data the product does not actually have.

## Landing page

Six sections, each a semantic `section` with its own `h2`, alternating canvas and surface backgrounds and ending on ink: hero → "for families / for attorneys" → how it works → benefits → privacy → closing call to action, then the shared footer. Copy lives under `landing.*` in `messages/`.

- **Hero:** caps eyebrow with the stamp mark, the headline, one subhead, both calls to action (black `lg` and ghost) and the honest note. The preview on the right is `LandingCaseMock` (`src/components/landing/`): a case detail built from the app's own primitives (a slice of the black top bar with the logo, `ReceiptNumber`, `StatusIndicator` at `lg`, the plain-language meaning, `KeyDateChip` with a pinned `now`, the "Shared with" row and `Timeline`) with sandbox data and a caption that says so. It is `role="img"` with a descriptive label, never a screenshot.
- **Audience split:** two columns from `md`, each with a caps label (icon in vermilion, the one accent of the section), a heading and four bullets with 32px icon tiles on the canvas.
- **How it works:** three steps in a subgrid so numbers, vignettes, titles and bodies line up across columns. Each step has a number on a hairline and a small vignette on the canvas built from real primitives (receipt and nickname fields; a status change with the "Changed" chip; a share row with "Active" and "Revoke"). Vignettes are `aria-hidden`; the text carries the meaning.
- **Privacy:** the mental model in two sentences (USCIS's status page is public and shows no personal data; Kinstamp is the family's private notebook on top, under row-level security, with anonymous insights only from people who opt in and only for groups of 5 or more) beside a two-card ledger: "What USCIS shows" and "What Kinstamp keeps private".
- **Closing:** an ink band with the title, a one-line "this is a sandbox demo" note, the `inverse` button and an underlined white link. No testimonials, no numbers.

Auth backdrop note: the stage markers are derived from the path's bezier data in `auth-backdrop.tsx` (segment midpoints and knots), not typed by hand, so they cannot drift off the dotted line. The traveller keeps the CSS `offset-path` copy of the same curve in `globals.css`; change both when changing the shape.
